USB capture

Read the PEC index and the worm period straight from the link between TheSkyX and the mount. A passive USBPcap capture (Wireshark) of the mount's USB serial adapter contains TheSkyX's own status polling: the HA encoder three times a second, and the PEC index once a second while the Bisque TCS window is on its Periodic Error Correction tab. Nothing is sent to the mount.

How to capture
  1. Install Wireshark with USBPcap ticked, and reboot once.
  2. With TheSkyX connected and the mount tracking, open the Bisque TCS window on the Periodic Error Correction tab and leave it showing.
  3. In Wireshark, double-click the USBPcap interface whose tooltip lists the mount's USB serial adapter. Let it run for at least five minutes, ideally through the whole Guiding Assistant run.
  4. Stop, save as .pcapng (or capture with USBPcapCMD to .pcap), and upload it here.

Ten minutes is about 50 MB. Uploads up to 1 GB are accepted.
